Privacy & limits

Conversation history stays in this tab and is sent to the selected service on each message. New chat clears local history and generated audio. Browser speech recognition may use your browser provider’s servers. Microphone permission is optional; text is never sent automatically.

Chat, voice and comparisons share a $10/month request budget and per-client limits. Saved-checkpoint comparisons use the same monthly allowance and may be slower or unavailable. Voice reads up to 2,000 characters per reply. Preparation can take about a minute or longer for long replies; progress is shown. Stop waiting and Resume check the same job without another generation or budget reservation. Request recovery lasts 24 hours; voice text and generated audio are also retained temporarily by the provider. Stopping disconnects this browser; already-started provider work may still incur cost.

AI voice is an authorized synthetic Rhys voice, not a recording of a new statement by Rhys. Public source excerpts can quote others; curated Anki and guest statements do not establish Rhys’s beliefs.
