Collected ≠ authored.

Anki notes can quote other people. Podcast guests have their own views. Transcripts can contain errors. Every excerpt keeps its source and authorship label; mixed or unverified speakers are excluded from the first Rhys adaptation recipe.

The initial instruction examples use actual study-note answers and verbatim writing continuations. They are a starting dataset, not proof that the model thinks like Rhys.

Privacy comes first.

Raw local archives are kept out of Git. Anki cards require individual semantic review before publication; unreviewed cards stay private. Dating, family, contacts, credentials, and other sensitive material are quarantined.

Public-source excerpts receive conservative automated screening. This is a partial release with known gaps and screening limitations. Read the dataset card or report a record.
