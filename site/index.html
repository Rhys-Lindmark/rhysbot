Collected ≠ authored.

Anki notes can quote other people. Podcast guests have their own views. Transcripts can contain errors. Every excerpt keeps its source and authorship label. HEE narration is attributed to Rhys based on his confirmation. Speaker turns were inferred from original audio for 214 recordings. Rhys labels use a voice reference cross-checked against his confirmed Digicene narration; other voices keep local guest labels. Anonymous or ambiguous identities remain uncertain. The unavailable Meta clip retains its earlier captions with unverified attribution. All podcast, YouTube and HEE transcripts are in this research corpus, but were excluded from both models’ pretraining and fine-tuning inputs. Speaker-aware training remains unfinished.

The initial instruction examples use actual study-note answers and verbatim writing continuations. They are a starting dataset, not proof that the model thinks like Rhys.

Privacy comes first.

Raw local archives are kept out of Git. Anki cards require individual semantic review before publication; unreviewed cards stay private. Actual personal-life material, private contact details, and credentials are withheld. We retained 20,980 of 21,301 cards; 321 were withheld.

Public-source excerpts receive conservative automated screening. This is a partial release with known gaps and screening limitations. Read the dataset card or report a record.
